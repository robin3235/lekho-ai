Frequently Asked Questions

Is Lekho.ai really free? +
Yes! All tools are completely free to use. You can generate up to 50 AI responses per day without any payment or registration.
Why does it say "daily limit reached"? +
To keep the service free for everyone, we limit each device to 50 AI generations per day. The limit resets at midnight. The font and unit converters have no limit at all.
The AI generated wrong information — what should I do? +
AI can sometimes make mistakes. Always review and edit the generated content before using it. For important documents like CVs, treat the output as a first draft and personalize it.
Does voice input work on all devices? +
Voice input works best on Google Chrome browser (desktop and Android). It may not work on some iOS browsers or older phones.
Is my data safe? +
Yes. We don't store your personal data on our servers. Your history is saved only on your device. Read our Privacy Policy for full details.
Can I suggest a new tool? +
Absolutely! Use the 💬 button on the main page or the form above to suggest new tools. We read every suggestion and add the most popular ones.
