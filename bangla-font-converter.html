Bijoy vs Unicode — What Is the Difference?

Bijoy is a legacy font encoding system developed in Bangladesh in the 1980s. It was the dominant way to type Bangla on computers for over 20 years. In Bijoy, Bengali characters are represented by remapping the English keyboard — pressing "K" types "ক", pressing "i" types "র", and so on. The most popular Bijoy font is SutonnyMJ. If you open an old Bijoy document without the SutonnyMJ font installed, you will see garbled English characters instead of Bengali text.

Unicode is the international standard for encoding text in any language. Unicode Bengali assigns a unique code to every Bengali character, so the text looks correct on any device, browser, or app — no special font installation required. All modern websites, Facebook, WhatsApp, Google Docs, and smartphones use Unicode Bengali.

Simple rule:
If you have old Bangla text that shows as jumbled English letters (like Avgvi †QvU †evb), it is Bijoy encoded. Use our Bijoy → Unicode converter to make it readable on any modern device.

Yes. The Lekho.ai Bangla Font Converter is 100% free to use with no daily limits, no registration, and no installation required. The Bijoy→Unicode, Unicode→Bijoy, and Banglish→Bangla converters all work instantly in your browser.
Why does my Bijoy text look like random English letters?
This happens because Bijoy uses the SutonnyMJ font encoding. Without that specific font installed, your computer shows the underlying ASCII characters instead of Bengali. Copy that garbled text and paste it into the Bijoy → Unicode tab to fix it.
Which newspapers and publications use Bijoy encoding?
Many older Bangladeshi publications including Prothom Alo, Daily Ittefaq, and Jugantor archives from before 2010 used Bijoy encoding. Government documents, legal papers, and academic certificates from that era are often in Bijoy or SutonnyMJ format.
Does this work on mobile phones?
Yes. The converter is fully mobile responsive and works on any smartphone browser. You can copy Bijoy text from a Word document or email, paste it here, convert it, and copy the Unicode output — all from your phone.
What is the difference between Bijoy and Avro?
Bijoy is a proprietary font-based encoding system — it requires special fonts to display. Avro is a free keyboard software that types in Unicode Bengali directly. Modern typing on computers in Bangladesh mostly uses Avro or mobile keyboards, but millions of old documents still exist in Bijoy format.
How accurate is the Banglish to Bangla conversion?
The converter uses a 200+ word dictionary for common Bengali words with perfect accuracy for known words. For words not in the dictionary, it applies phonetic rules automatically. Accuracy is very high for everyday words and sentences. Complex technical or rare words may need manual correction.
