What is Lekho.ai?

Lekho.ai (লেখো) means "Write" in Bengali. We built this platform because most AI tools are in English, expensive, or not designed for how Bangladeshis actually use technology.

Whether you are a student who needs help writing an assignment, a job seeker who wants a professional CV, a small business owner who needs product descriptions for Daraz, or someone who just wants to write a beautiful poem for someone they love — Lekho.ai has a tool for you.

Everything is free. No registration required. Works on your mobile phone. Supports Bengali and English.
